How to play

Drag a gear from the tray onto an empty peg (or tap a gear, then a peg). Gears that touch mesh and turn in opposite directions. Connect the crank to every target gear so it turns in the arrow's direction, without touching rusty gears. Tap the green crank button to test your train.
